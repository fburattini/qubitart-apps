Privacy Policy

For the mobile game Nobody Comes Back.
Published by Filippo B. · qubitart.help@gmail.com
Effective 4 November 2026.

This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game itself collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. The one exception is the advertising it shows, which is served by Google, and that is what most of this page is about.

What the game collects: nothing

Nobody Comes Back has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting. It makes no network requests of its own.

Your progress — the current run, your unlocked characters and your settings — is saved on your own device and stays there. It is never uploaded, never transmitted and never seen by the developer. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which is the only third party in the app and the only party that receives any information about you.

To select and measure ads, Google collects and shares the following:

WhatUsed for
Your device's advertising identifierSelecting ads, measuring them, preventing fraud
Interactions with ads, such as views and tapsMeasuring ads, preventing fraud
Interactions with the app, such as launchesMeasuring ads, preventing fraud
Diagnostic information, such as launch time and crashesReliability and performance of the ads
Your approximate location, estimated from your IP addressServing ads relevant to your region, and meeting local law

The advertising identifier is the only thing here used to track you across other apps and websites, and only when you have allowed it (see below). None of this reaches the developer: it goes to Google, and Google shares it with its advertising partners. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The developer does not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before it requests a single ad. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time: tap the cog in the corner of the start menu, then PRIVACY.
  • Tracking, on iOS. iOS asks separately whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used for that, and you will still see ads — they will simply be less relevant. You can change your answer in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Resetting the identifier. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device's own settings, on either platform.

Children

This game is not directed to children. Its subject matter is intended for an older audience, and it is not designed, marketed or rated for children's use. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, write to the address below and it will be dealt with.

Keeping and deleting information

The developer holds no information about you, so there is nothing held to request a copy of, to correct, or to delete. Your saved progress lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

For information Google holds in connection with advertising, Google is the party that holds it. Your rights over it, and how to exercise them, are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Withdrawing consent and resetting your advertising identifier, both described above, are the two controls that take effect immediately in the game itself.

Where information goes

Google operates internationally, so information it collects for advertising may be processed outside your own country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Changes that affect what is collected, or why, will also be reflected in the game's store listing before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in this game, go to qubitart.help@gmail.com.
