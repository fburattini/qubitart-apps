Write to the address above with anything at all — a bug, a run that went wrong, a question, or a suggestion. It is read by the person who made the game.

What helps when reporting a problem

  • Your device and its operating system version, for example "iPhone 13, iOS 18.2".
  • What happened, and what you expected instead.
  • Where in the game it happened — the menu, a particular chapter, a boss fight.
  • A screenshot or a screen recording, if you have one.

Common questions

My progress is gone. Progress is saved on your device only, so it does not survive deleting the app, moving to a new phone, or restoring from a backup that predates it. There is no account to sign in to and no copy held anywhere else.

How do I change my privacy or ad consent? Tap the cog in the corner of the start menu, then PRIVACY. The privacy policy explains what the ads collect and what the controls do.

Can I turn the music or sound off? Yes — the cog on the start menu has toggles for music, sound and haptics.

The game is very hard. It is meant to be. Runs are short and losing one is the usual outcome; characters and expeditions unlock as you play.
